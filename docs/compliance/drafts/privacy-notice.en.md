## Who can use GroundKart

GroundKart is for adults aged 18 and over. We ask you to confirm you are 18 or older when you create an account or book as a guest. Please do not enter the personal details of children (for example names or ages of children attending an event) in bookings, reviews, messages or tickets. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we suspend it and erase its data, except records the law requires us to keep.

## How we use your data

We use the data above only for the purposes in the table: running your account, handling bookings, payments, refunds and complaints, verifying venue owners and paying them, publishing listings and verified reviews, keeping GroundKart secure, and meeting our legal duties. If we ever want to use your data for a new purpose, we will ask you first.

## Who we share it with

- **Venue owners.** When you book a venue, we give its owner your name, contact details, booking details and notes so they can host your event. Venue owners handle this data for your booking under their own responsibility and our Terms, which allow them to use it only for your booking.
- **Service providers who process data for us:** our hosting provider [[OWNER TO FILL: hosting provider and data-centre region]], which stores our database, files and logs. We have or will have a written contract that requires them to protect the data and delete it when asked.
- **Banks and UPI providers** that handle the payments and refunds you make or receive.
- **Authorities**, only when the law requires it, for example a court order or a request from CERT-In or the Data Protection Board.

We do not share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

### External services you may choose to open

Some pages offer links or players from other companies. Nothing is loaded from them until you click: a venue's video (YouTube or Vimeo), "Open in Maps" (Google Maps) and share links (WhatsApp). When you click, that company receives your IP address and handles it under its own privacy policy.

## How long we keep it

We keep data only for as long as the table above says, then erase or anonymise it. Some booking and payment records must be kept for up to 8 years under tax and accounting laws even after you close your account; we keep only what those laws need and record why. Deleted data may remain in our backups for up to 14 days before the backups expire.

## Your rights

You can:

- **Get a summary** of the personal data we hold about you and who we shared it with: download it from **My Data** in your account.
- **Correct or update** your name, phone or email in your profile, or ask us to correct anything else.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time from **My Data**. It is as easy as giving it: one button. Withdrawing closes your account; it does not undo what we did lawfully before, and we keep only the records the law requires.
- **Ask us to erase** your data from **My Data**. We confirm when it is done.
- **Nominate** someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself, from **My Data**.
- **Complain** to our Grievance Officer (below). If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the **Data Protection Board of India** through its website or app.

If you booked as a guest, use the private link we gave you, or write to the Grievance Officer with the booking reference and the email you used. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

**How fast we answer:** we acknowledge requests and complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days. Complaints about content posted on GroundKart (listings, photos, reviews) are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 15 days. In every case we answer within the 90-day maximum set by the DPDP Rules, 2025.

## How we protect your data

- All traffic uses HTTPS, with HSTS so browsers refuse an insecure connection.
- Passwords are stored only as Argon2 hashes. Sign-in tokens are kept in memory and a secure, httpOnly cookie, never in browser storage.
- Identification numbers, PAN, GSTIN and bank account numbers are encrypted in our database and shown masked.
- Identity documents are kept in private storage that is not reachable from the web; only admins can open them, and every view is logged.
- Admin accounts must use two-step sign-in. Access is limited by role: customers see only their own bookings, owners only their own venues.
- Sign-in, sign-up and form submissions are rate limited to stop abuse.
- We keep security and audit logs for 1 year, take daily backups, and have a written plan for data breaches.
- If a breach affects you, we will tell you without delay with what happened, what it means for you and what you can do, and we will report it to the Data Protection Board and to CERT-In as the law requires.
