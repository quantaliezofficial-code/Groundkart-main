# Cookie and Third-party Notice

> DRAFT FOR LAWYER REVIEW. This notice has been prepared by the GroundKart team and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

Version 1.0 · Last updated 10 November 2026

GroundKart uses no analytics, advertising, tracking or social media cookies, and no tools that follow you across websites. That is why we do not show a cookie banner: there is nothing to switch on or off except the optional setting below.

## The one cookie we set

| Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `refresh_token` | Keeps you signed in. Sent only to our sign-in service, cannot be read by page scripts (httpOnly), sent only over HTTPS. | 30 days, or until you sign out | Strictly necessary |

## Settings stored in your browser

These stay on your device and are never sent to anyone else.

| Key | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `groundkart_active_role` | Remembers whether you were last using GroundKart as a customer or venue owner |
| `groundkart_favorites` | The venues you saved |
| `groundkart_referral_code` | A partner referral code from a link you opened, so it can be filled in when you list a venue |
| `groundkart_external_media` | Your choice to load venue videos automatically (off unless you switch it on) |
| Search filters, scroll positions, dashboard tab (session only) | Puts you back where you were; cleared when you close the tab |

## Fonts and images

All fonts and images are served from GroundKart's own servers. Nothing is fetched from font or image services when you open a page.

## Third-party services that load only when you click

| Service | When | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube (`youtube-nocookie.com`) or Vimeo | When you press play on a venue video, or if you switch on "Load venue videos automatically" | Your IP address and browser details; the provider may set its own cookies |
| Google Maps | When you click "Open in Maps" | The venue's location and your IP address |
| WhatsApp | When you click a share button | The text of the message you choose to share |

Each of these companies handles the data under its own privacy policy.

## Changing your choice

Use **Cookie settings** in the footer of any page to switch automatic video loading on or off, or to see this list again. You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser at any time; you will be signed out.
