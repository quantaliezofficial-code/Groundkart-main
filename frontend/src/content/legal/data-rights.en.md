# Your Data Rights

> DRAFT FOR LAWYER REVIEW. This procedure has been prepared by the GroundKart team and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

Version 1.0 · Last updated 10 November 2026

You have rights over the personal data GroundKart holds about you. Signed-in users can use all of them from **My Data** in the account menu. Each request gets a reference number and a due date that you can see on the same page.

| Right | How to use it | What happens | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Summary of your data | My Data → Download my data | A file (JSON) with your profile, bookings, tickets, reviews, consents and the list of who received your data | Immediately |
| Correction | Profile → edit name, phone or email; for anything else, My Data → Ask for a correction | We update the record and tell anyone we shared the wrong data with | Profile: immediately. Requests: within 30 days |
| Withdraw consent | My Data → Withdraw consent | We stop processing on the basis of consent and close your account. Records the law makes us keep (payments, refunds, tax) are kept with the reason recorded | Immediately |
| Erasure | My Data → Delete my data | We erase or anonymise your account, contact details, booking notes, tickets, reviews (shown as "Former customer"), notifications and uploaded files, and ask our service providers to do the same. You get a confirmation | Within 30 days (usually at once) |
| Nominee | My Data → Nominee | Name someone who can use these rights for you if you die or become unable to act. They will need to show proof of your death or incapacity and their identity | Saved immediately |
| Grievance | Contact form or the Grievance Officer | See [Grievance Redressal](/grievance) | 48 hours to acknowledge |

## If you do not have an account

If you booked as a guest, write to the Grievance Officer (see [Grievance Redressal](/grievance)) with your booking reference and the email you used. We confirm it is you by asking for details only the booker knows, such as the booking reference, date and phone number.

## What we may ask for

To protect your account we may ask you to sign in again, or to confirm the phone number or email on the account before acting on a request. We never ask for your password or card details.
