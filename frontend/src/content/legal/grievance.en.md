The Grievance Officer handles complaints about bookings, payments and refunds, venue listings, reviews and other content, and how we handle your personal data. They are also the person who answers questions about our processing of personal data.

## How to complain

1. Use the [Contact form](/contact) and choose the closest topic, or email the Grievance Officer.
2. You get a ticket number straight away (for example GK-T-2611-0042). Keep it to follow up.
3. Signed-in users can follow the ticket under **Help and support**. Guests can quote the ticket number when they contact us; our team replies to the email or phone number given.

Please include your booking reference if the complaint is about a booking, and do not include card numbers or passwords.

## How long we take

| Type of complaint | We acknowledge within | We resolve within |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content on GroundKart (a listing, photo, video or review) | 24 hours | 15 days |
| Bookings, payments, refunds and anything else | 48 hours | 1 month |
| Requests about your personal data (summary, correction, erasure, nominee, consent) | 48 hours | 30 days, and never more than the 90 days allowed by the DPDP Rules, 2025 |

If we need more information we will ask on the ticket; the time you take to reply is not counted.

## If you are not satisfied

- Bookings and payments: you can contact the National Consumer Helpline on 1915 or file a complaint before the Consumer Commission with jurisdiction under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
- Personal data: you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India after using our grievance process.
