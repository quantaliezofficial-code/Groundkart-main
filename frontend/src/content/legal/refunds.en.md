# Refund and Cancellation Policy

> DRAFT FOR LAWYER REVIEW. This policy has been prepared by the GroundKart team and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. Items in double square brackets are business details still to be filled in.

Version 1.0 · Last updated 10 November 2026

This policy applies to every booking made on GroundKart. The cancellation windows below are read from our live booking settings, so the numbers on this page are always the ones the system uses.

## Cancelling a booking request that is not yet confirmed

You can withdraw a request at any time before it is confirmed, free of charge. Unconfirmed holds are released automatically after {{hold_minutes}} minutes.

## Cancelling a confirmed booking

| When you cancel | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| {{full_refund_hours}} hours or more before the start time | 100% of the amount paid |
| Between {{partial_refund_hours}} and {{full_refund_hours}} hours before the start time | {{partial_refund_percent}}% of the amount paid |
| Less than {{partial_refund_hours}} hours before the start time, or after the start | No refund |

Cancel from **My Bookings** (or from your guest booking page). The refund amount is calculated and shown before you confirm the cancellation.

## If the venue owner or GroundKart cancels

If the venue owner cancels, or we cancel because the venue cannot host your booking, you get a **100% refund**.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds are paid back by the mode you paid ([[OWNER TO FILL: accepted payment modes]]) within [[OWNER TO FILL: refund processing time]] of the cancellation being approved. You can see the status on the booking.

## Rescheduling

You can ask to move a confirmed booking to another available slot from **My Bookings**. The venue owner decides whether to accept; you will see the decision on the booking.

## Things that do not apply to venue bookings

GroundKart sells time at venues, not goods. There is no shipping or delivery, no return or exchange of goods, and no product warranty. Anything included with a venue (equipment, decoration, catering) is described on its page and covered by the venue's rules.

## Problems with a booking

If the venue was not as described or your booking was not honoured, raise a ticket from the booking or use the [Contact form](/contact) as soon as you can after the event. We acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within one month. See [Grievance Redressal](/grievance).
